FAQ

Questions before we start

How long does a project take?

Most brand websites take two to four weeks. Web apps and systems are scoped after discovery, depending on complexity and integrations.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. The process works remotely with clear milestones, async updates and structured review points.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. I can audit the current experience, clarify what should stay, then rebuild the interface and conversion journey around the new direction.

Do you handle design and development?

Yes. Strategy, interface design, frontend development and launch preparation are handled in one workflow.

What happens after launch?

We test the live experience, monitor the essentials and define the next iteration if the project needs ongoing growth work.

How do we start?

Send a short project brief through the form or WhatsApp. I will review the goals, ask the right questions and propose the next step.
